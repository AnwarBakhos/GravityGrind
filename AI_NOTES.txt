For AI I mainly used Claude throughout this project, working with it a bit like a small studio where we both shared the director chair. The game idea, the two modes, ghost racing and my inspirations (Ballest of Them All, Maze-Tilt Game, The Tilt) came from my one-pager. From my descriptions and examples, AI helped me find and gather the free Kenney art and sound packs and set them up in the project. AI organized all my C# code into scripts by job (game flow, ball physics, level building, ghosts, sound, the level editor), it aided me with comments explaining each part so I am always up to date with my code even the pieces I get online. That let me keep spitting out ideas and testing them while not getting lost or overwhelmed. 

I also used AI to help me get the project onto GitHub, since I struggled with moving it from Unity to Git. Outside of AI, I got a lot of help from Discord and from friends in lab :)
